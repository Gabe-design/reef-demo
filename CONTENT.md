## Not on the public site yet (on purpose)

- **Reviews**: no testimonials, star ratings or review counts until Reef has real reviews it can publish.
- **Before/after gallery**: needs Reef's own job photos with customer permission. The slider component already exists in the customer portal.
- **Licensed & insured, years in business, guarantees**: add only once Reef confirms them.

## Sample data inside the apps

Customers, addresses, jobs, payments, reviews, texts and team members in the dashboards are all generated sample data. Team names are fictional; the owner account is labeled "Reef Owner" until we have the owner's name.
